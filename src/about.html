About & Attribution

Strata Learning Graph · An open, graph-structured taxonomy of skills for ages 4 to 15.

Project Notice & Heritage

Created by: Samiul Islam

Project Fork: Forked from the Marble Skill Taxonomy by Marble.

Attribution: Marble Skill Taxonomy (v1) · © Generative Spark, Inc. (Marble) · withmarble.com

Dataset Stats: v1 · 1,591 skills · 3,225 prerequisite relationships · 8 subjects

Strata Learning Graph organizes learning for ages 4 to 15 into teachable, interconnected skills. Skills are connected in a directed acyclic graph to make clear what to learn first and what comes next, with many skills mapped to curriculum standards.
